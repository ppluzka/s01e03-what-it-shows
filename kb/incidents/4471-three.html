owner Piotr Pluzka open reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

#4471 merged every date report into one known issue and called it the parser. Reading the reports splits that ticket.

The shared parser reads a date month first. These tenants write the day first, so the month and the day flip.

On booking-v3 the form prints MM/DD/YYYY. The form then reads the date the way the tenant writes it, day first. People follow the hint, and the day flips the other way. That bug is the hint.

The desk wrote down the reports it read as a one-day move, and still filed them under the parser. A one-day move shifts the date by a day.
